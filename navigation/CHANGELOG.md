# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Changed

- Needs the CMS 0.4 or later.
- **Navigation** is in the sidebar's Content section, and a menu is saved from the save bar that shows while it has changes.
- With no menus yet, the list shows its message without a second **New Menu** under the one in the top bar.
- **Delete Menu** is at the end of a menu’s page, with what it means, and the line of explanation under its name is gone.
- Permissions are **View Menus** and **Create and Edit Menus**.
- How deep a menu may go is **Submenus**: No Submenus, One Level, 2 Levels…, instead of an unlabeled 1–5, and the menu list says “3 links”.
- A link’s buttons (move, options, remove) are always in view, not only on hover.

### Fixed

- **Save** goes gray again when you put a menu back the way it was saved.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-02)
