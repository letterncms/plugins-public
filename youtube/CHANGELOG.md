### Added

- **YouTube** block: a video or Short by its link or id. The page shows its poster with a play button and loads YouTube only when a visitor plays it, so pages stay fast and no cookie is set before. With a start time, subtitles in the site's language, privacy-enhanced mode, a choice of shape, and a poster of your own if you like. A private video, or one that is gone, shows nothing, and the editor says why.
