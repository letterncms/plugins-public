## Unreleased

### Added

- **Search Field** and **Search Results** blocks, for sites people mostly search. The field goes on any page, looks like the site's other fields and buttons, and sends what was typed to the results page, also without JavaScript. The results page finds every word typed, the start of a word as well ("recycl" finds "recycling"), with or without accents, puts title matches first, and shows each result's kind, date and a snippet with the words marked. Filters by kind of page ("Service", "News") show when the results have more than one, and "Show More Results" adds the next ones. Screen readers hear how many were found. Pages are found by a **Keywords** field too, when their collection has one, so a page on waste collection can also be found as "bin".
