# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- The chat works beside the page, on every screen. It opens next to the sidebar's icons with `⌘J` or from the bottom of the sidebar, the page stays open and usable, and anything it changes shows there as it happens, in the builder too. Drag it wider, or take it to full size and back without losing your place.
- The chat knows what you're looking at, including what's on the page: the entry you have open, the block selected in the builder, the text you selected. Chips in the composer show what a message carries, and each comes off with its ×. Account, Users, Licence and Audit Log stay private.
- Each message shows the page it was sent from, one click away.

### Changed

- Your chats are in one place: in the chat, by age, with a column of their own at full size. They're no longer listed in the sidebar or the Ask widget.
- **New Chat** is no longer a sidebar row: the chat's place is at the bottom of the sidebar, under the same pin.
